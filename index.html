Meet Your Coach

About Me

Born and raised in Queens, NY, Pedro began his fitness journey in 2010. Like many, the COVID-19 pandemic threw a wrench into his routine, resulting in significant weight gain. Determined to take control, Pedro committed to consistent workouts and smart nutrition, ultimately losing 60 pounds and transforming both body and mindset.

Through years of personal experience, Pedro discovered that heavy lifting isn’t always the safest path—injuries taught the importance of training smarter. Now, he focuses on hypertrophy-based programs to build muscle effectively while staying injury-free. Passionate about helping others reach their goals, Pedro brings empathy, personal insight, and evidence-backed strategies to every client session.
